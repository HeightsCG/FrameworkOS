REMOVE CREATOR SELLING + ONLYFANS-STYLE POSITIONING + ADULT CONTENT  (2026-11-09)
====================================================================================

WHY
Stripe stopped allowing live charges on the platform account ("Your account cannot currently make
live charges"). To pass Stripe review the product must stop being / looking like a fan-subscription
(OnlyFans-style) or adult platform. Daniel's decisions (2026-11-09):

  1. DELETE creator-to-fan selling from the codebase entirely (not hide).
  2. Remove every comparison to OnlyFans / Fansly / Fanvue / Patreon / Passes / Ko-fi etc.
  3. Existing Subscribers-only and Pay-per-view posts are ARCHIVED (never made public).
  4. Events, Services and CLS Video (LiveKit calls, tips, replays) are DELETED completely.
  5. ALL adult content goes: adult toggle, Didit AGE verification, adult starter scenes,
     is_adult handling, every "adult content allowed" line. Adult posts are archived.
     (Didit IDENTITY verification for the creator badge is NOT adult — keep it.)
  6. Plans keep Free/Creator/Studio prices; the "% of sales" fee row disappears everywhere;
     plans are pitched on AI credits, AI influencers, scheduling, automations, team seats,
     custom domains, analytics.

THE ONLY PAID FUNCTIONS (owner, 2026-11-09): the plan subscription (Free/Creator/Studio incl. its
add-ons such as extra influencer slots) and AI credit purchases (AI packs + monthly AI pack).
Everything else that takes or pays money goes. Affiliates are paid by staff by hand (stays).
