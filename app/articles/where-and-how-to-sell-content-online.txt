## What does it actually cost to sell content online in 2026?

Selling content online in 2026 costs you three separate things, and most creators only count the first.

### The three costs stacked on every sale

The first cost is the platform take rate: OnlyFans takes 20% of each fan payment, per the [OnlyFans terms of service](https://onlyfans.com/terms), and Patreon takes 10% on pages published after August 4, 2025, per [Patreon's creator fees overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview). Payment processing comes on top. Then come the monthly subscriptions for the tools around the store: a link-in-bio, a scheduler, a checkout. Stack them and your effective take rate climbs well past the headline number.

### A worked $2,000 month on a stitched-together stack

Say you earn $2,000 a month in memberships and PPV. A 10-20% platform fee takes $200-400. Payment fees skim another few percent, and a link-in-bio tool, a scheduler and a store add $30-60 a month in subscriptions, roughly another 2-3% of revenue. You keep closer to 75-85%, not the 80-90% the platform fee implies.

The fix is fewer tools and a lower combined fee. Our guide to [monetizing your content](/monetize-your-content) shows how Creator Link Studio puts the bio page, scheduler and store in one place.

## What content types can you sell online, and which platforms fit each?

The content types you can sell online each pay out differently, so match the format to the platform and the take rate, not the other way around.

### Video and pay-per-view

[Pay-per-view](/features/pay-per-view) video is where platform fees bite hardest, because the ticket price is high and the percentage comes off every sale. OnlyFans and Fanvue both take 20% on PPV messages and posts.

### Photo sets and bundles

Bundled photo sets behave like PPV: one price, one fee, so a lower take rate compounds fast if you sell weekly. Sell bundles straight from your link-in-bio so fans buy without leaving your funnel.

### Memberships, tiers and free trials

Recurring [memberships](/features/memberships) reward whichever platform charges the least, since fees stack over 12 months. Patreon fits free-following audiences; OnlyFans and Fanvue fit paid-walled audiences. Run tiers with a free trial to pull fans in, then upsell PPV on top.

### Services, events and 1:1 calls

Services and 1:1 calls have the widest margins, so platform choice matters less than checkout speed. Creator Link Studio sells services and events with built-in video calls; Beacons and other storefronts also sell digital products such as courses.

## Can you sell content online free, and what do free tiers actually cost you?

Yes, you can sell content online without paying a monthly fee, but "free" almost always means the platform takes a bigger cut per sale or locks features behind an upgrade. The question is not the monthly bill, it's the take rate on every PPV, bundle and tier you sell.

### When a free tier is worth it

A free tier is the right call when you're testing a niche, building a free trial funnel into a paid membership, or earning very little so far. If you're pre-revenue, a 10-20% platform fee on almost nothing still costs almost nothing. Free link-in-bio plans also work fine while your audience lives on one platform.

### What free tiers leave out

Free tiers often gate scheduling, payouts or custom domains, which pushes you into three stitched-together tools anyway. Creator Link Studio's Free plan lets you set up your page, but selling starts on a paid plan, and custom domains come with Studio.

## Where can you sell content online if you already have an audience vs. starting from zero?

Where you sell content online depends on where your traffic comes from. If you already bring traffic from social, use a storefront or link-in-bio tool where you control the checkout. If you are starting from zero, pick a platform with discovery that can surface you to strangers.

### You already have traffic: own the checkout

When your Instagram or X followers are the source, a discovery feed adds nothing except a take rate. You want a page that converts warm visitors: memberships, PPV drops, bundles, and payouts to your bank. Creator Link Studio runs that model, and Beacons plays the same link-in-bio role.

### Starting from zero: discovery-first platforms

Fanvue has built-in discovery and OnlyFans has a referral program, and both mechanics can put unknown creators in front of new fans. The trade is a bigger platform fee for audience you could not reach alone.

## Which is the best place to sell content online for subscriptions vs. one-time sales?

The best place depends on your posting rhythm. If you ship new photo sets or clips on a predictable schedule and fans stay for months, a subscription model compounds: every week of retention is another payment without new selling work.

### Subscriptions win on recurring photo and video cadence

Membership tiers work when each month has obvious new value: fresh sets, behind-the-scenes drops, priority DMs. If your answer to "what do subscribers get this month?" is thin, churn eats the recurring revenue. Patreon and OnlyFans are built for this cadence.

### One-time wins on PPV, bundles and drops

Pay-per-view and bundles beat a monthly tier when you post irregularly or sell big-ticket items. Bundles let buyers rebuy deliberately. A hybrid setup, like Creator Link Studio's membership free trials plus per-purchase sales, covers both without stacking fees.

## How do you sell your own content online without giving up ownership or control?

Selling your own content on any hosted platform means the platform can suspend your account, hold a payout under review, or license your content in ways you did not expect. The OnlyFans terms of service, for example, grant the platform a broad licence to the content you upload and cover account termination. Read the terms before you upload, not after a dispute.

### Read the terms before you upload

Three things matter most: whether you keep copyright, whether the platform can repost or licence your content elsewhere, and what happens to pending payouts if your account is closed. Patreon, Fanvue, OnlyFans and Beacons all differ on these points, so check each one you currently pay a take rate to.

### Keep an export habit

Download your media files and your subscriber list to your own storage every week. Creator Link Studio runs your link-in-bio, scheduler and store in one place, but Creator Link Studio is still hosted software: export your files and audience contacts regularly, because no platform, including ours, removes the need for your own backup.

## How do you move your content if a platform changes terms or shuts down?

Moving your content starts with an exit plan built before you need it. Export your fan list monthly if the platform allows it, keep original copies of every photo, video and PPV bundle outside the platform, and download your revenue history so you can prove income when you migrate.

Your link-in-bio is the one asset that should never break. Host your bio link on a tool you control, like Beacons or Crownerr, so a platform switch means changing one link, not losing the traffic you paid for.

## FAQ

### Where can I sell content online and keep the most money?

Keep the most money by driving your own traffic and using a lower-fee storefront. Creator Link Studio charges a 10% platform fee on the Creator plan and 3% on Studio, versus the 20% OnlyFans and Fanvue take. ### Can I sell content online for free?

Yes, most platforms offer a free tier, but free usually means a bigger cut per sale or gated features like scheduling and custom domains. Free makes sense while you test a niche or earn very little. ### How do I sell my content online without a big following?

Start on a discovery-first platform. Fanvue has built-in discovery and OnlyFans has a referral program, which can put unknown creators in front of new fans. Build a free-following audience on Patreon if your format suits it. Once you have traffic, move sales to a link-in-bio storefront where you keep more per sale.

### What is the best place to sell content online for adult-adjacent creators?

OnlyFans and Fanvue are the strongest fits for discovery and referrals, and both allow adult content that mainstream tools restrict. The 20% fee buys some traffic. If you already bring your own audience and want to keep more of each sale, run your own storefront alongside them.

### How do I sell online content if I already post on OnlyFans or Fanvue?

Add a direct storefront as a second revenue channel rather than replacing the platforms. Use your existing audience to sell PPV drops, bundles and memberships where the fee is lower, and keep the discovery platforms for new fans. Export your fan list and media regularly so a terms change or shutdown never costs you the audience.
